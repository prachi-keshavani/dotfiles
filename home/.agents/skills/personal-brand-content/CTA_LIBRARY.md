# 90-Day CTA Library

Platform-specific calls-to-action for every day of the 90-day content plan. Use these to drive comments, saves, shares, and follows on TikTok and LinkedIn.

---

## How to Use This Library

- **Story days**: Ask for experiences and emotions. These get the most comments.
- **Lesson days**: Ask for tips, frameworks, and opinions. These get saves and shares.
- **Framework days**: Ask for action, tags, and saves. These build authority and bookmarking.

## CTA Strategy: Why These Work

### TikTok Growth Mechanics

| Signal | What It Does | Which CTAs Trigger It |
|--------|-------------|----------------------|
| **Comments** | Tells algorithm people want to discuss | Open questions, "Drop a [emoji]", "Who else has been here?" |
| **Saves** | Highest value signal; means replay value | "Save this if...", "Save this framework", "Bookmark for later" |
| **Shares** | Expands reach beyond followers | "Tag someone who...", "Who needed to hear this?" |
| **Watch time** | Completion rate boosts distribution | Short, reflective CTAs that don't feel like an abrupt ending |
| **Profile visits** | Converts viewers to followers | "I read every comment", "Part 2 is on my profile" |

**TikTok Rules:**
1. End with energy, not a sales pitch.
2. Ask ONE question. Two questions split the response.
3. Use "I read every comment" to build loyalty and return viewers.
4. "Tag someone" posts get shared 2-3x more than solo posts.
5. "Save this" drives the algorithm harder than likes.

### LinkedIn Growth Mechanics

| Signal | What It Does | Which CTAs Trigger It |
|--------|-------------|----------------------|
| **Comments in first hour** | Triggers broader distribution | Direct questions, "Agree or disagree?", "What would you add?" |
| **Dwell time** | Longer reads = more reach | White space, one strong number, CTA at the end keeps them scrolling back |
| **Reactions + comments combo** | Higher weight than likes alone | Questions that invite expertise or disagreement |
| **Reshares with thoughts** | Gold standard for reach | Contrarian or framework posts that people want to attach their take to |
| **Follows from post** | Converts reach to audience | Consistent series so people follow for the next installment |

**LinkedIn Rules:**
1. One question per post. Make it impossible to answer with just "yes."
2. Ask for experiences, not opinions. "What happened to you?" beats "Do you agree?"
3. Framework posts get saved and reshared. Story posts get commented on.
4. Reply to every comment in the first 60 minutes. This is non-negotiable for reach.
5. End carousels with a question on the last slide, not a logo.

## Weekly CTA Rotation Tips

**Monday (Story Day):** Lead with emotion and experience. Ask "When did you...?" or "What surprised you?" These get the most comments because everyone has a story.

**Wednesday (Lesson Day):** Lead with insight. Ask "How do you...?" or "What is your framework?" These get saves because people bookmark practical tips.

**Friday (Framework Day):** Lead with action. Ask "Tag someone" or "Save this." These get shares and saves as people prep for the weekend or send to colleagues.

**Sunday (optional bonus):** Repurpose the week's best-performing Story into a carousel. Use a "What did I miss?" CTA to harvest more engagement before Monday starts.
