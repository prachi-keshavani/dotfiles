## What Makes Her Content Work

### 1. She Always Asks for the Save

**Every. Single. Video.** says "save this," "save for later," or "🏷️ Save these for later!"

This is her signature. On TikTok, saves are weighted **heavier than likes** in the algorithm. She treats saves as the primary CTA, not comments or follows.

**Your adaptation:**
- End every framework/tutorial video with "Save this if you're [specific situation]."
- Use the 🏷️ emoji as a visual save cue.
- Make content worth saving: step-by-steps, checklists, book lists, frameworks.
