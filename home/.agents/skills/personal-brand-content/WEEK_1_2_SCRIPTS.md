# Week 1-2 Scripts: Exact Talking Points, Timing & Visuals

> These are shot-by-shot scripts. Film in one take when possible. Use CapCut for captions and cuts.
>
> **Timing rules:**
> - Hook: 0-3 seconds (spoken + on-screen text simultaneously)
> - Body: 3-50 seconds
> - CTA: last 5-10 seconds
> - Total: 45-75 seconds for stories/lessons/frameworks. 60-90 seconds for tutorials.

### DAY 1 — MONDAY — Story: 1,200 Applications

**Format:** Talking head, close-up (chest up), natural window light
**Length target:** 60 seconds
**Background:** Plain wall or bookshelf. No clutter.

**0:00-0:03 — HOOK**
- **Spoken:** "I applied to 1,200 jobs before I understood how hiring really works."
- **On-screen text (large, centered):** 1200 JOBS. 0 EASY WINS.
- **Visual:** Direct eye contact with camera. Slight pause after hook.

**0:03-0:20 — BODY**
- **Spoken:** "I was an international student in the UK. Working side jobs. Sending applications like it was a full-time job. I thought volume would win. It didn't. Rejections from the UK, Oman, Dubai, and Mumbai."

  - "UK ✗" 
  - "Oman ✗" 
  - "Dubai ✗" 
  - "Mumbai ✗"

  - "No LinkedIn presence"
  - "No proof of work"
  - "No warm conversations"

**0:45-0:55 — CLOSE**

- **On-screen text (bottom):**

  - "2. Proof of work"
  - "3. Warm conversations"
- **Visual:** Hold up three fingers, one at a time.

**0:55-1:00 — CTA**

- **On-screen text (bottom, bold):** SAVE THIS 👇 DROP YOUR FRUSTRATION

## Shooting Checklist (Use Before Every Video)

- [ ] Phone fully charged
- [ ] Storage cleared (at least 2GB free)
- [ ] Natural light source in front of face, not behind
- [ ] Microphone clear of obstructions (test with clap)
- [ ] Background clean and uncluttered
- [ ] Hook practiced 3 times before hitting record
- [ ] On-screen text planned in advance
- [ ] CTA memorized so it flows naturally
- [ ] One take minimum, three takes maximum (don't overthink)
- [ ] Film vertical (9:16) for TikTok

## Editing Checklist (CapCut)

- [ ] Import footage
- [ ] Auto-captions ON (large font, high contrast)
- [ ] Cut all pauses longer than 1 second
- [ ] Add on-screen text at hook (first 1 second)
- [ ] Add key text overlays for numbers/frameworks
- [ ] Add background music at 5-10% volume (optional but recommended)
- [ ] Export at 1080p, 60fps if possible
- [ ] Post with caption + hashtags + CTA
