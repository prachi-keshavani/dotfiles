# Reference: Content Engine & Brand DNA

## Core Positioning

Help ambitious professionals build better careers through data skills, visibility, networking, personal branding, and opportunity creation.

**Target audience**: International students, early-career marketers, data/analytics professionals, job seekers, and first-gen immigrants building careers abroad.

**Platforms**: TikTok (short-form video, authentic talking head) and LinkedIn (professional text posts, carousels, repurposed video).

## Content Rotation Engine

Use a 3-day cycle. Each cycle can focus on one career moment.

| Day | Phase | Structure |
|-----|-------|-----------|
| 1 | Story | What happened. Grounded, specific, one moment. |
| 2 | Lesson | What I learned. Insight extracted from the moment. |
| 3 | Framework | What I would tell someone else. Actionable system or tip. |

**Monthly focus**:
- Month 1 = Story-heavy (build emotional connection)
- Month 2 = Lesson-heavy (build credibility)
- Month 3 = Framework-heavy (build authority and utility)

## Platform Specifications

### TikTok

| Element | Guideline |
|---------|-----------|
| Length | 60-90 seconds ideal. Up to 3 minutes for deep frameworks. |
| Hook | First 1-3 seconds. Spoken + on-screen text. Specific and slightly surprising. |
| Structure | 1) Hook 2) Story/Problem 3) Insight 4) One takeaway 5) CTA |
| Captions | Simple, large text. Cut pauses. Keep pace natural. |
| Visual | Talking head mostly. B-roll of LinkedIn, dashboards, or city scenes where relevant. |
| Posting | One video per day during active 90-day pushes. |
| Best CTA | End with one thought you want people to remember. Ask one reflective question. |

### LinkedIn

| Element | Guideline |
|---------|-----------|
| Format | Text post (short or long-form), carousel, or native video. |
| Hook | First 2 lines must earn the "...see more" click. |
| Structure | Short paragraphs with white space. One specific number or outcome per post. |
| Tone | Professional but personal. Same honesty as TikTok, slightly more polished framing. |
| Carousels | 5-10 slides. 1 concept per slide. Strong cover slide with hook. Last slide = CTA. |
| Best CTA | One question to start a conversation in comments. |

## Voice & Style Rules

1. **Direct and honest** — no generic motivational fluff.
2. **Reflective** — write like explaining your career to someone who actually cares.
3. **Specific numbers** — "£8K in four months," "1,200 applications," "£25K in one month."
4. **Grounded close** — end with what you would actually do now, not theory.
5. **Unpolished is okay** — delivery should feel human, not like a creator template.
6. **Closed mouths don't get fed** — recurring theme: ask, show up, speak before ready.

## Hook Formulas

Use these as starting points, then customize with a specific number or moment.

- "I [did X] before I understood how [Y] really works."
- "Most people [common behavior]. I [different behavior] instead."
- "[Specific number] [outcome]. Here is what I actually learned."
- "I [unexpected action] with no prep. That changed everything."
- "[Contrarian statement that contradicts generic advice]."
- "I was [undesirable situation] before I [turning point]."

## CTA Bank

**TikTok CTAs**
- Leave one sentence about what you would do differently now.
- Turn the lesson into one practical tip people can use now.
- Keep the close grounded: one line about what you would actually do now.
- What is one skill you are building right now that will matter later?
- If you have lived this, drop one line below.

**LinkedIn CTAs**
- What is one thing you wish you knew earlier in your career?
- What is a stopgap job that taught you more than you expected?
- How do you turn boring work into a skill that compounds?
- What is one framework you use to break down overwhelming data?
- If you are navigating this now, what is your biggest question?
